Having trouble with FocusTube? Try these quick steps first:

  • Refresh the YouTube page.
  • Restart your browser and try again.
  • Make sure FocusTube is enabled.
  • Temporarily disable other YouTube-related extensions to check for conflicts.
  • If the issue continues, reinstall FocusTube and try again.

When reporting an issue, please include:

  • A brief description of the problem.
  • A screenshot or screen recording, if possible.
  • Your browser and version (e.g., Chrome 88.0).
  • Your FocusTube version (e.g., v1.0.0).

The more details you provide, the easier it is to identify and resolve the issue.
